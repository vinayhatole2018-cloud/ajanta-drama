Welcome to Ajanta School of Drama and Film Trust

Established on December 18, 2006, the Ajanta School of Drama and Film Trust is dedicated to the preservation, promotion, and enrichment of Indian art and culture. Based in Aurangabad, Maharashtra, our work spans theatrical education, street plays, skill development, and social welfare programs.

Through the medium of performing arts, we aim to bridge social divides, provide platforms for marginalized talent, and spread awareness about crucial social issues like healthcare, women's empowerment, and environmental preservation.

Our Core Values

🎭

Artistic Excellence

Imparting professional education in acting, directorship, stage design, and set modeling.

📢

Social Change

Using street plays (Pathanatya) to educate public on health, agriculture, and civic duties.

🤝

Inclusive Growth

Providing free theater education and mental wellbeing support to orphaned and handicapped children.
