Our Activities & Social Impact

Promoting Indian classical art, empowering fading Marathi traditions, and serving society through drama, film education, and social theater.

🎭 Education

Theatrical Education & Drama

We organize professional workshops in acting, scriptwriting, lighting design, and direction. Students learn both traditional styles and modern techniques, paving their path into the professional film and drama industry.

📢 Awareness

Street Plays (Pathanatya)

Our street plays serve as a powerful tool to address social issues such as health, family welfare, water conservation, and agricultural reforms, while simultaneously keeping fading Marathi folklore and theater traditions alive.

🤝 Welfare

Special Needs & Orphan Support

We believe art is therapeutic. We offer specialized drama workshops, storytelling sessions, and support structures to boost confidence and build life skills in orphaned, poor, and handicapped children.

🧘 Wellness

Yoga & Vipassana Programs

Promoting mental health and wellness through free sessions of Yoga, Pranayama, and Vipassana meditation for the local community and student groups.
