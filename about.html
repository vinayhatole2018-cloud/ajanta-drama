About Our Organization

Promoting Indian classical art, empowering fading Marathi traditions, and serving society through drama, film education, and social theater.

Our Legacy

Since our registration in 2006, we have actively served Maharashtra's cultural and social landscape. Our focus has been to revive traditional folk performance, provide scholarships to underprivileged artists, and train the next generation of actors and directors.

Key Objectives

  • Promotion of Indian art and culture, drama, music, dance, and singing.
  • Providing scholarships and creative platforms for talented rural artists.
  • Imparting professional education in acting, directing, set design, and theatrical arts.
  • Public education on social issues, healthcare, agriculture, and fading Marathi cultural traditions through street plays (Pathanatya).
  • Spreading health and mental peace through Yoga, Pranayama, and Vipassana.
  • Providing drama education, therapy, and support for handicapped, poor, and orphaned children.
